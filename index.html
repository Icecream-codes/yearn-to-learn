Who we are

Every little step makes a difference.

Yearn to Learn Training and Consulting is a proudly black female owned, BBBEE Level 1 organisation focused on assisting clients to have compliant and effective SHEQ Management Systems.

We offer a wide array of services to organisations of all sizes and types, including accredited training programmes, compliance audits and SHEQ Management System implementation, among others.

We are of the view that every little step taken towards compliance and excellence makes a difference, so we pride ourselves on advising and assisting our clients accordingly at all levels.

Our services

Competent, professional SHEQ services.

Our services are delivered by a team of highly-qualified and experienced consultants. As a compliant service provider, we guarantee the highest standards in service that ensure results.

  • SHEQ Consultation Service

    Practical advice on safety, health, environment and quality, at every level of your organisation.

  • Accredited Training Programmes

    Training programmes that build real competence across your workforce.

  • ISO Management System Implementation

    Management systems built around how your business actually runs.

  • Auditing Services
